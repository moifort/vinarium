# App Store — localized listing content (to paste into App Store Connect)

The French listing lives in `APP_STORE_LISTING.md`; this file holds the six added
App Store locales. All six are declared in App Store Connect since 1.4, and the
descriptions below match what is published byte for byte. Keywords are the main
ASO lever and are indexed per locale (do not repeat the app name or category
words; comma-separated, no spaces).

App name stays "Vinarium" in every locale except English: Apple refuses that name
in en-US because another app holds it there, so the English stores list the app as
"Vinarium Wine Cellar".

Wine terminology in the translations should get a native review pass before
submission.

`en-GB`, `pt-BR` and `es-MX` are written below and are being declared in the 1.5
cycle. A locale can only be added while the version is not in review, so they go in
before the build is submitted — see Phase 2 of
[APP_STORE_SUBMISSION.md](./APP_STORE_SUBMISSION.md).

## English (en-US)

**Subtitle (≤30):** Your smart wine cellar

**Promotional text (≤170):** Scan a label and AI fills it all in: estate, vintage, price. Organize your cellar, track your tastings and share it with your household.

**Keywords (≤100):** cellar,wine,sommelier,vintage,tasting,bottle,collection,scanner,grape,winery,cave

**Description:**
Vinarium is your wine cellar in your pocket.

Photograph a bottle's label: the AI recognizes the wine and automatically fills in the estate, vintage, region, appellation, and even estimates its price. No more typing everything by hand.

FEATURES
• AI scan: snap a label, get a complete record in seconds
• Grid cellar: arrange your bottles by row and column, find them at a glance
• Journal: a history of entries and exits, with your tasting notes and ratings
• Dashboard: your cellar's value, occupancy rate, bottles to drink soon, recent activity
• Powerful search: by name, producer, region, vintage or person, with combinable filters
• Ready to drink: Vinarium alerts you when a bottle reaches maturity
• Favorites: mark the wines, spirits or beers that stayed with you, and find them again in one tap
• Where you found it: the scan records the place you were in, so you remember where a bottle came from
• Gifts & recommendations: keep track of wines given, received, or recommended by a friend

NOT ONLY WINE
A port, a rum, a whisky, a sake, a champagne or a beer has its place in the cellar too, with the details that belong to its kind of bottle.

HOUSEHOLD SHARING
Share a single cellar with the people in your household through an invitation code. Everyone can store, move or drink any bottle in the common cellar, while keeping their own library, notes and journal private.

RESPECT FOR YOUR PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking. Your data stays yours and can be exported at any time.

Sign in with a single tap using Sign in with Apple and start building your cellar.

VINARIUM PREMIUM
The cellar, the journal and household sharing are free and unlimited. Only the label scan is metered: five scans a month. Premium lifts that limit and adds the estimated price, the drinking window and the region to every scanned bottle. Premium also holds several cellars, each with its own grid.

Premium comes as a monthly plan or a yearly plan that opens on a 7-day free trial; both prices are shown in the App Store. The subscription renews automatically unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. It is managed and cancelled from the App Store account settings.

**Keywords (≤100):** ワイン,ワインセラー,ソムリエ,ヴィンテージ,テイスティング,ボトル,コレクション,スキャン,ブドウ品種,セラー

**Description:**
Vinariumは、ポケットの中のワインセラーです。

ボトルのラベルを撮影するだけで、AIがワインを認識し、生産者、ヴィンテージ、地域、アペラシオンを自動で入力し、価格まで推定します。もう手入力は必要ありません。

主な機能
• AIスキャン：ラベルを撮影すれば、数秒で完全な記録が完成
• グリッドセラー：ボトルを行と列で整理し、ひと目で見つけられます
• 履歴：入出庫の記録に、テイスティングノートと評価を添えて
• ダッシュボード：セラーの価値、使用率、まもなく飲み頃のボトル、最近のアクティビティ
• 強力な検索：名前、生産者、地域、ヴィンテージ、人物で、絞り込みを組み合わせて
• 飲み頃：ボトルが飲み頃を迎えるとVinariumがお知らせ
• お気に入り：心に残ったワイン、スピリッツ、ビールに印を付けて、ワンタップで呼び出せます
• 出会った場所：スキャンした場所を記録し、そのボトルとどこで出会ったかを残します
• ギフトとおすすめ：贈った、もらった、友人にすすめられたワインを記録

ワインだけではありません
ポート、ラム、ウイスキー、日本酒、シャンパン、ビールもセラーに並びます。種類ごとの情報も一緒に。

世帯での共有
招待コードで、世帯の人と1つのセラーを共有できます。共有セラーのどのボトルも、誰でも収納・移動・消費できます。各自のライブラリ、メモ、履歴は個別のまま保たれます。

プライバシーの尊重
広告なし、トラッキングなし。あなたのデータはあなたのもので、いつでもエクスポートできます。

「Appleでサインイン」でワンタップでログインし、セラーづくりを始めましょう。

VINARIUM PREMIUM
セラー、履歴、世帯での共有は無料で無制限です。カウントされるのはラベルスキャンのみで、月に5回まで無料です。Premiumはこの上限をなくし、スキャンした各ボトルに推定価格、飲み頃、地域を追加します。また、Premiumでは複数のセラーを持つことができ、それぞれにグリッドを設定できます。

Premiumには月額プランと、7日間の無料トライアルから始まる年額プランがあります。価格はApp Storeに表示されます。期間終了の24時間前までに解約しない限り、サブスクリプションは自動更新されます。管理と解約はApp Storeアカウントの設定から行えます。
